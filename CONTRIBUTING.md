# Contributing

Work follows the [Verified OSS Loop](https://github.com/kvnloo/verified-oss-loop).

## Claims

- A claim lease lasts 72 hours by default and no more than 168 hours.
- Each contributor may hold one active claim.
- There is one canonical issue per problem. Search before proposing or claiming work.
- Claims confer a temporary right to work, not authority to merge, deploy, or change the roadmap.

## Deliverables and evidence

Put all deliverables under `contributions/<issue>/`. Bind the pull-request receipt to the exact base and head revisions. Disclose AI assistance in the receipt's `ai_assistance` field. An independent reviewer must not be the pull-request author. Maintainers make every merge decision.

## Safety

Do not collect or commit sensitive personal data. Do not contact affected people, deploy software, spend money, or change an external system without explicit maintainer approval. Report vulnerabilities through GitHub private vulnerability reporting; never publish a security report in an issue.
