# Current-events intake

Review situation pages published in the last 24 hours by UN OCHA, WHO, UNHCR, and ICRC. Propose no more than three bounded tasks using every field from the Humanitarian task issue form.

- Cite primary sources and preserve their publication dates and URLs.
- Search open and closed issues first; keep one canonical issue per problem.
- Apply `task` and `needs-triage`, never `claimable`.
- State jurisdiction, intended user, falsifiable hypothesis, source-grounded insights, deliverables, and safety constraints.
- Do not include sensitive personal data, contact affected people, deploy, spend, or change external systems.

Maintainers alone decide priority and whether a proposal becomes claimable.
